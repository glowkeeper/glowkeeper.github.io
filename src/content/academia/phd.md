# PhD

I completed my PhD in Informatics at the [University of Sussex](https://www.sussex.ac.uk/) in 2020. My research asked how blockchain technology might be applied beyond cryptocurrencies to social, economic and environmental challenges.

Using design science research, I built and evaluated a series of software artefacts that explored provenance, decentralised collaboration, shared-economy applications and incentives for more sustainable behaviour. The work found genuine potential in decentralised technologies while recognising that technology alone cannot resolve problems rooted in political, economic and cultural choices.
