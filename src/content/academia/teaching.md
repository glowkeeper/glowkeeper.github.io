# Teaching

I teach computing, data and academic practice in universities and on intensive industry bootcamps. My work spans teaching, curriculum design and the production of complete learning resources: lectures, workshops, assessments, tutor guidance and practical projects. In 2025, I completed a Postgraduate Certificate in Higher Education (PGCertHE) and became a Fellow of Advance HE (FHEA).

## University of Roehampton

I am a Visiting Tutor at the [University of Roehampton](https://www.roehampton.ac.uk/). I have taught Academic and Communication Skills (ACS) and Data Analysis and Research Skills (DA&RS), helping students develop the research, analytical and communication skills they need for university study.

I have also designed teaching content for two modules:

- [Data Visualisation and Intelligence (DVI)](https://www.roehampton.ac.uk/study/academic-areas/continuing-education/extended-masters/), a postgraduate module covering data visualisation, visual analytics, AI-assisted analysis, dashboard design, decision support and the responsible use of AI
- [Data Analysis and Research Skills (DA&RS)](https://www.roehampton.ac.uk/study/foundation-studies/computing-international-foundation-pathway/), including learning materials, practical activities and assessment support

This work involves more than classroom delivery. I develop coherent curricula and create the materials needed to support both students and tutors throughout a module.

I have also delivered an [introductory workshop on building 3D games in Unity](https://github.com/glowkeeper/IntroToBuilding3DGamesInUnity) for Foundation Year students. The workshop introduced 3D space, materials, physics and scripting through a small practical game project.

## Software Development Bootcamps

At [Creative Process](https://creativeprocessdigital.com/), I design and teach eight-week, beginner-facing software development bootcamps. Learners progress from planning and developer workflow through HTML, CSS and JavaScript to React, browser persistence, testing, deployment and a final project showcase.

I produce the [bootcamp curriculum and teaching resources](https://github.com/glowkeeper/software-development-bootcamp), including tutor guidance, slide decks, worksheets, learner activities and progressively developed reference applications. The programme uses practical client-informed briefs and gradually shifts responsibility from guided demonstration to independent development. It also teaches learners to use AI critically as a mentor while retaining understanding and ownership of their work.

## Programming for 3D

From 2020 until 2025, I was Module Convenor for Programming for 3D at the [University of Sussex](https://www.sussex.ac.uk/). I designed and delivered an intensive Unity-based module for third-year undergraduate and postgraduate students, combining technical game development with creative and professional practice.

The module covered creative thinking, narrative design, agile project management and source control alongside 3D modelling, materials, shaders, lighting, physics, scripting, multimedia, animation, particle systems and cutscenes.

## Full-Stack Web Development

From 2020 to 2021, I taught on a six-month full-stack web development bootcamp run by [Boolean UK](https://boolean.co.uk/). The course took learners from HTML, CSS and responsive interfaces through JavaScript and React to server-side development with Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, SQL and Prisma.
