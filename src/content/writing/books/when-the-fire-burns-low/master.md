# When the Fire Burns Low

## Tales of Magic, Mischief and Unexpected Wisdom

By Steve Huckle, Ed Burton and Matt Quinn

Every July, [Brighton Buddhist Centre](http://www.brightonbuddhistcentre.co.uk/) held a family gathering at the [Rivendell Retreat Centre](http://www.rivendellretreatcentre.com/), near Uckfield in East Sussex, UK. It was a beautiful event that ran for many years.

On the final evening, all the families met for a barbecue at the retreat campfire. Afterwards, we ate toasted bread that the children baked in the fire, sang songs and told stories. Then we headed out through the dark woods and across a narrow bridge over a small stream for a ‘midnight walk’.

It became a tradition that, during the week, someone would write the main story to be told by the campfire. This book is a collection of the stories written between 2010 and 2016.
