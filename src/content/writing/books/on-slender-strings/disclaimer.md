# Disclaimer

*On Slender Strings* tells a true story. Andrew Langston is a pseudonym for the father and is the first-person narrative voice throughout, including in the appendix.

The children, their mother and some other people also appear under pseudonyms, and some identifying details have been altered or omitted to protect their privacy. The chronology and medical events are presented as faithfully as memory and the available contemporary records allow. Conversations were not recorded. Dialogue has been reconstructed from recollection, correspondence or those records; quotation marks do not signify a verbatim transcript.

Nothing in this book should be interpreted as medical or legal advice.
