# Disclaimer

*On Slender Strings* is a memoir. It recounts events from my own life as I remember them and reflects my perspective. Within the narrative, I have called myself Andrew Langston.

The names of my children, their mother and some other people have also been changed. Some identifying details have been altered or omitted to protect their privacy. The chronology and medical events are described as accurately as I can, drawing where possible on contemporary records. Conversations, however, were not recorded: dialogue in quotation marks represents my best recollection of what was said and should not be read as a verbatim transcript.

Nothing in this book should be interpreted as medical or legal advice.
