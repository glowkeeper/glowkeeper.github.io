# Dedication

I dedicate this book to the many children either with or who have had brain tumours. May their stories inspire us all to take up their cause, so their trials are not in vain.

This book is also dedicated to the healthcare professionals who work in the field of paediatric neuro-oncology. They care a great deal. I hold them in the highest regard; they have both my respect and my utmost gratitude for the incredible work they perform in the most challenging of circumstances.
