# Epilogue: Five Years Later

*August 2021.*

It is five years since Abi finished chemotherapy. She has thrived ever since finishing that treatment, and she is as happy and healthy as I have ever known her. Indeed, we just got the results of Abi's last scan, and because everything is stable, her oncologist has discharged her back to the care of our local services and has discontinued regular scans. In fact, Abi's oncologist thinks it is unlikely he will see Abi ever again. So even though that's fantastic news because the implication is that Abi's cancer is behind her, the meeting was tinged with sadness. His setting us free feels like some of my foundations have been removed, somehow. So I felt torn and very emotional at our goodbye.

Meanwhile, my thoughts are with everyone beginning this journey. If you are one of those, then I know how difficult things may appear right now, but hang in there - many of you will reach the same place as us.
