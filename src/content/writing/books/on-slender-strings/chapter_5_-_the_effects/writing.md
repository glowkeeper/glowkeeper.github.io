# Writing

I quickly realised that I needed to find a way to express my worries over Abi’s illness. I found my way through writing about it. I kept a diary.

I began writing by accident. When Abi first got ill, I responded the only way I knew how; by falling back on my computer training and creating a website about her illness. There, I started a blog and recorded everything that happened. I found the whole process of documenting our journey incredibly rewarding. It also proved a wonderful way of keeping family and friends informed of everything going on.
