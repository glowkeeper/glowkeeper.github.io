# Appendix

This appendix separates what I understood when Abi was ill from what is known in 2026. Some pages preserve the information I had at the time and then explain what has changed. Others correct things I got wrong or did not explain properly in the original edition.

The [Glossary](/writing/books/on-slender-strings/appendix/glossary) explains medical and legal language used throughout the book. The medical pages cover Abi’s tumour, treatment and outcomes; the statistical pages put incidence and funding claims into context; and the final group revisits the alternative and legal questions I wrestled with at the time.

Every factual page now ends with a **Sources and Further Reading** block. A complete, subject-by-subject list is also available on the [Sources and Further Reading](/writing/books/on-slender-strings/appendix/sources_and_further_reading) page.

I hope the appendix makes the book easier to understand and its factual claims easier to check. It is background to one family’s story, not medical or legal advice.
