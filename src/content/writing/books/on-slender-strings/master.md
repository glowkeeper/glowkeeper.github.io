# On Slender Strings

## A Father’s Story of Love, Fear and a Childhood Brain Tumour

By Steve Huckle

*On Slender Strings* is a father’s honest account of his daughter’s childhood pilocytic astrocytoma, from the first missed symptoms and emergency brain surgery through recurrence, chemotherapy and survival—and the unexpected distance between them that followed. The original chronological record is preserved alongside a 2026 appendix correcting outdated information and explaining how diagnosis, treatment and evidence have changed.
