# On Slender Strings

## A Father’s Story of Love, Fear and a Childhood Brain Tumour

By Steve Huckle

*On Slender Strings* tells the true story of a father whose young daughter was diagnosed with a pilocytic astrocytoma, from the first missed symptoms and emergency brain surgery through recurrence, chemotherapy and survival. The original chronological record is preserved alongside a 2026 appendix correcting outdated information and explaining how diagnostic practice, treatment options and the medical evidence have changed. The father appears in the book under the pseudonym Andrew Langston.
