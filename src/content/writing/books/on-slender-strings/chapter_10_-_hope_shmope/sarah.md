# Sarah

*Written in 2016.*

During Abi’s illness, her mum and I have lurched from one argument to another. Even six years after the divorce, we have been unable to resolve our differences. In fact, our fights are getting worse.

Adversity either brings people together, or it rips them apart. Most divorced couples are eventually able to create some distance from each other, but Abi’s illness has required Sarah and me to spend hours together at our daughter’s bedside. Under that pressure, old wounds have repeatedly reopened. I know how quickly I can push Sarah’s buttons, and I have not always been good at stepping away when I should.

I cannot place all the blame for that on Sarah. The sad fact is that I reached my early thirties, panicked that I was getting old, and married without properly understanding either myself or what I could give to another person. We reap what we sow, after all. Whatever went wrong between us, we still have to find a way to be Abi’s parents.
