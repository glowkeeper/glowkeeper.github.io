# My Birthday

It’s my birthday! To celebrate, Nadia, Abi and I have been out for a meal at our favourite Italian restaurant. We’ve just had cake; unfortunately, it wasn’t quite big enough for the correct number of candles, so, instead, I used two and let the girls blow out one each. Afterwards, they gave me their presents; Nadia bought me a cuddly hippo, Abi got me a fluffy owl. I’ll get revenge one Christmas by buying them a PlayStation.
