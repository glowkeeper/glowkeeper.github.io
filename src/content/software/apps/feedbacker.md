# Feedbacker

[Feedbacker](https://feedbacker.education/) is a free, open-source tool that helps university educators use AI to mark and moderate coursework against a rubric and brief, while keeping every judgement human. The AI suggests; the educator decides. It never assigns a final mark, and nothing reaches a student without the educator's approval.

For marking, you can import a cohort's submissions from your marking platform, choose whether to see the AI's suggested level for each criterion before or after you mark, and have it draft feedback from your own marks and comments, checking that draft for things such as praise your marks don't support. For moderation, you mark a colleague's sample yourself, then compare your marks with the original marker's and the AI's, criterion by criterion.

There are no accounts and no Feedbacker server. It runs on your own computer, and your files stay in folders you control. Student names, IDs and contact details are anonymised locally, and only what you approve is sent to the AI provider, using your own API key.
