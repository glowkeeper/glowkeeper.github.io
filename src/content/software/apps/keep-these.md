# Keep These

[Keep These](https://keepthese.com/) is a calm, private-by-default tool for making blackout poetry from a carefully curated shelf of classic writing. Choose the words that speak to you and let the rest fall away; the app never selects words or composes the poem for you.

There are no accounts, analytics, advertising trackers or uploads. Work is saved in your browser, images are generated on your device, and every passage retains its attribution and a route back to the original text.

[Make a poem at keepthese.com](https://keepthese.com/).

[View the open-source project on GitHub](https://github.com/glowkeeper/keepthese).
