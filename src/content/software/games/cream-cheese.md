# Cream Cheese

[Cream Cheese](https://creamcheese.fun/) is a daily numerical deduction game—a modern interpretation of the classic *Pico, Fermi, Bagels*. A secret three-digit number waits behind the counter, and you have twenty guesses to work out what it is.

Each guess earns a clue rather than an answer. *Fermi* means a right digit in the right place, *Pico* a right digit in the wrong place, and *Bagels* that none of those digits appears at all. Nothing tells you which digit produced which clue, so the deduction is entirely yours to do.

There are two ways to play. *Today's Bagel* is the same puzzle for everyone and refreshes at midnight UTC; *Another Helping* offers unlimited practice puzzles generated in your browser. There are no accounts, no tracking and no leaderboards—daily progress and play statistics stay on your own device.

The logic is taken seriously; almost nothing else is. *Bagels* is credited to D. Resek and P. Rowe, and was published by David H. Ahl in *101 BASIC Computer Games*. Cream Cheese is an independent modern tribute, built with new code, writing and design.

[Play Cream Cheese](https://creamcheese.fun/) or [learn how to play](https://creamcheese.fun/how-to-play/).
