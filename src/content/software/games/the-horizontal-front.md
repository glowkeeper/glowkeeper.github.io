# The Horizontal Front

[The Horizontal Front](https://thehorizontalfront.org/) is a satirical rhythm game about working extremely hard to do absolutely nothing. Management wants you vertical. You want to stay exactly where you are. Hold the line to a rhythm of rubber stamps, typewriters and pneumatic tubes while a grotesque embodiment of capitalism hauls on a brass lever bolted to the head of your bed.

The satire is pointed: the working day treats your rest as an unclaimed resource and your attention as inventory. Staying horizontal is gruelling, skilled, rhythmic work. Under that arrangement, even lying down is labour. That contradiction is the argument.

The game is built as a [digital commons](https://thehorizontalfront.org/commons/)—free to play, no advertising, no tracking, no purchases, copyleft code and share-alike cultural work, playable offline. New episodes can be authored by anyone against a [published grammar](https://github.com/glowkeeper/the-horizontal-front/blob/main/docs/episode-grammar-reference.md), without writing any code.
