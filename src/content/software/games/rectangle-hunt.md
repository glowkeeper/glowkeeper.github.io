# Rectangle Hunt

[Rectangle Hunt](https://rectanglehunt.com/) is an untimed daily visual-search game about finding rectangles hidden in line drawings. Take your time, look closely and see how many you can discover.

Everyone gets the same daily drawing, with a new hunt arriving at midnight UTC. Your progress and discoveries are saved on your device, so you can return to an unfinished hunt and share a spoiler-free invitation when you're done.

The game grew out of an exploration of Exercism's rectangle-counting problem. Practice drawings in the how-to-play guide offer a place to learn the hunt before tackling the daily puzzle.

[Play Rectangle Hunt](https://rectanglehunt.com/) or [learn how to play](https://rectanglehunt.com/how-to-play).
