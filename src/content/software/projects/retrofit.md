# Retrofit

[My Victorian Retrofit Home](https://retrofit.huckle.studio/) tells the story of retrofitting the Victorian mid-terrace cottage on the south coast of England where I have lived since 2011. It covers what I changed, why it matters, what it has brought me, what it cost, and the obstacles I hit along the way. The aim is simple: to make eco-retrofitting an older house a little easier to understand.

You can wander through a 3D model of the house, built from photographs and rough measurements, which serves as the setting for the stories. It isn't a survey, and I wouldn't build from it. If you'd rather skip the model, every story is also available as plain text, and there is an accessibility statement, a glossary and a page on who did the work.

It grew out of my [MSc](/academia/msc) in sustainability, energy and the built environment, and it is where the theory met a draughty Victorian cottage.
